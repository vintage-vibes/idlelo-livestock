The Attorney Behind The Practice

A Journey of Service & Justice.

he journey began in 1991 with a career in the South African Police Service. Through dedication, discipline, and continued professional development, he progressed through the ranks, ultimately serving as a Captain. His years in the police force provided valuable experience in leadership, law enforcement, and understanding the complexities of the legal system.

His career also took him across much of South Africa as a journalist, broadening his perspective and giving him the opportunity to engage with people and communities from different backgrounds. Throughout this journey, education remained a central part of his life. He completed courses including Terminal Operations, Circulation, Street Survival, and Legal Principles, continually expanding his knowledge and professional capabilities

His commitment to education ultimately led him to pursue a degree in Law through the University of South Africa (UNISA), which he obtained on 19 October 2012. After years of service, experience, and study, he took the next step in his legal career by opening his own legal practice in August 17 2022.

Today, that journey continues. Built on decades of experience, discipline, education, and service, his practice remains committed to providing clients with professional legal guidance and dedicated representation.

Mathew Nkosinathi Gule Founder & Legal Practitioner
