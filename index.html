The Attorney Behind The Practice

A Journey of Service & Justice.

The legal journey of Mr Mathew Nkosinathi Gule began in 1991 when he joined the South African Police Service (SAPS) as a Student Police Officer. Through dedication, discipline and continuous professional development, he progressed through the ranks and ultimately promoted to Detective Captain and served at ladysmith as a court case officer in the Detective and Court Case Department (DCCO) .

While serving in the police force, Mr Gule continued to pursue his education and professional development. Despite the demanding nature of police work and the many challenges he encountered along the way, he remained committed to advancing his knowledge and skills. His academic achievements include a Diploma in Policing, Bachelor of Technology in Policing, Honours-level studies, and a Bachelor of Laws (LLB).

His professional development was further strengthened through specialised training and courses, including Detective Training, Stock Theft Investigation, Detective Investigation, Crime Prevention, Computer Studies, Circulation and Terminal Operations, Street Survival, and Legal Principles.

After more than two decades of service, Mr Gule resigned from the police service in 2015 to begin a new chapter in his professional life — his journey into the legal profession.

He joined Maree & Pace Mpulo as a Candidate Attorney, where he completed his articles while continuing his academic development. He attended the University of KwaZulu-Natal (UKZN) in Durban, obtained the necessary certificates and successfully completed his board examinations.

During his time as a Candidate Attorney, Mr Gule gained valuable courtroom experience and successfully represented numerous clients. His dedication to his clients and commitment to his work earned him recognition not only in Ladysmith, but also in surrounding towns.

Following the completion of his legal training, Mr Gule was admitted by the High Court of South Africa as an Attorney of the High Court. This achievement marked an important milestone in his career, but for Mr Gule, it was only the beginning of an even greater commitment to the legal profession and to serving his community.

In 2022, he established his own legal practice called Gule Nathi Attorneys inc situated in 70C Queen Street, Ladysmith, near AVBOB. Through hard work, perseverance and an unwavering dedication to his clients, the firm has grown rapidly and earned a strong reputation within a relatively short period of time.

As the practice continues to expand, Mr Gule has built a dedicated and reliable team, including Ms Asiphe Mapholoba, Attorney, Ms Thabile Xaba, C/Attorney, and Ms Carol Magabo, C/Attorney.

Together, the team works with dedication, integrity and determination to provide professional legal assistance and guidance. Their approach is founded on the belief that every client deserves to be heard, represented and supported throughout their legal journey.

From beginning his career as a Student Police Officer in 1991 to becoming an experienced attorney and founder of his own practice, Mr Mathew Nkosinathi Gule’s journey has been shaped by discipline, perseverance, continuous learning and an unwavering commitment to serving others.

Today, Mr Gule and his team continue to work tirelessly to protect the rights and interests of their clients, remaining committed to the highest standards of professionalism and legal excellence — still going strong, and the journey continues.

Mathew Nkosinathi Gule Founder & Legal Practitioner
